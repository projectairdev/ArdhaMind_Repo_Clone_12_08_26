# UI Design Rules — ArdhaMind Workstation

---

## 1. Design Aesthetics

- **Institutional Dark Theme**: Tailored HSL color palette (`#08090B`, `#0E1013`, `#13161A`, `#191D23`, `#38BDF8`, `#00C896`, `#E59700`, `#E5484D`).
- **Compact & High Information Quality**: Dense, decision-oriented layout designed for active trading workstations. Avoid excessive whitespace or low-density placeholder cards.
- **Typography**: Clean monospace / sans-serif fonts for data tables and indicators.

---

## 2. Information Hierarchy

```text
Market Overview ──► Opportunity ──► Confidence ──► Trade Setup ──► Risk ──► Action
```

- **Progressive Disclosure**: `Glance -> Inspect -> Deep Dive`. Keep top-level overview simple while offering full detail on click/hover.
- **Trader-Facing Terms**: Use institutional trader language (`Expected Open`, `Floor Pivot`, `Decision Corridor`, `Max Pain`, `Call Wall`, `Put Wall`).
- **No Redesign Without Approval**: Maintain accepted workstation layouts.
