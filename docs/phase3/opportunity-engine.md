# Phase 3.1 — Opportunity Detection Foundation

## 1. Overview & Objectives

ArdhaMind Phase 3.1 introduces a deterministic, real-time NIFTY opportunity detection, scoring, qualification, ranking, and lifecycle tracking engine.

> **Primary Objective:** Continuously answer: What legitimate NIFTY trading opportunities exist right now, which ones are becoming actionable, which ones are blocked or invalid, and which good opportunities did ArdhaMind miss?

### Core Invariants
- **READ-ONLY Guarantee:** Phase 3.1 is strictly informational. No broker order placement, position modification, paper trading execution, or capital risk authorization.
- **Zero-Synthetic Data:** All intelligence is derived exclusively from canonical market, option, news, and macro runtime data.
- **Staging-Only Isolation:** Phase 3.1 is strictly scoped to staging and does not alter production execution or infrastructure.
- **Deterministic & Auditable:** Detection, scoring, qualification, and lifecycle transitions are 100% deterministic. Optional LLM interpretation only summarizes canonical state.

## 2. Architecture & Lifecycle Flow

```text
MARKET DATA (Spot, VWAP, Support/Resistance, Option Chain, Breadth, News)
    ↓
5 INDEPENDENT DETECTORS (Breakout, Breakdown, Pullback, Reversal, Momentum Continuation)
    ↓
RAW CANDIDATES (With explicit DetectorResult evidence & level suggestions)
    ↓
MULTI-COMPONENT SCORING (13 sub-scores → Quality, Confidence, Priority 0-100)
    ↓
QUALIFICATION ENGINE (Session, Freshness, R:R Thresholds, Signal Conflicts)
    ↓
DEDUPLICATION ENGINE (Price-binned fingerprint grouping to prevent tick duplicate creation)
    ↓
CONTINUOUS RE-EVALUATION & LIFECYCLE TRACKER (RAW → WATCHING → QUALIFIED → TRADE_READY / BLOCKED / REJECTED / INVALIDATED / EXPIRED)
    ↓
OPPORTUNITY REGISTRY SERVICE (In-runtime stateful singleton with bounded retention)
    ↓
BEST OPPORTUNITY SELECTION (Deterministic ranking → BEST TRADE NOW or NO_TRADE)
    ↓
CANONICAL STATE INTEGRATION (`opportunity_intelligence` field on CanonicalWorkstationState)
    ↓
FRONTEND "AI OPPORTUNITIES" WORKSPACE & DETAIL DRAWER
```

## 3. First Detector Set

Phase 3.1 implements five independent detectors conforming to the `OpportunityDetector` contract:

### A. Breakout Detector (`breakout` - v1.0.0)
- **Target:** Bullish breakouts above validated resistance / intraday structure.
- **Inputs:** `current_spot`, `resistance_levels`, `vwap`, `atr`, `pcr`, `advances`, `declines`.
- **Evidence:** Spot price clearing resistance + ATR buffer (prevents single-tick penetration triggers), holding above VWAP, bullish trend alignment, put writing exceeding call writing, positive market breadth.

### B. Breakdown Detector (`breakdown` - v1.0.0)
- **Target:** Bearish structure breakdowns below support.
- **Inputs:** `current_spot`, `support_levels`, `vwap`, `atr`, `pcr`, `advances`, `declines`.
- **Evidence:** Spot price breaking key support - ATR buffer, holding below VWAP, bearish trend alignment, call writing exceeding put writing, declining market breadth.

### C. Pullback Detector (`pullback` - v1.0.0)
- **Target:** Healthy pullbacks within an established trend.
- **Inputs:** `current_spot`, `vwap`, `atr`, `pcr`, `support_levels`, `resistance_levels`.
- **Evidence:** Controlled retracement near VWAP (within 0.5x ATR), holding above key support (bullish) or below resistance (bearish), primary trend strength >= 40%.

### D. Reversal Detector (`reversal` - v1.0.0)
- **Target:** Directional reversals requiring strict multi-factor agreement.
- **Inputs:** `current_spot`, `vwap`, `atr`, `india_vix`, `pcr`, `support_levels`, `resistance_levels`, `advances`, `declines`.
- **Evidence:** Sharp structural rejection at key levels, failed breakout/breakdown, breadth divergence, heavy options positioning shift. Reversal threshold requires raw score >= 65 and at least 3 concurring evidence points.

### E. Momentum Continuation Detector (`momentum_continuation` - v1.0.0)
- **Target:** Acceleration in an already-established move without chasing overextended price.
- **Inputs:** `current_spot`, `vwap`, `atr`, `pcr`, `advances`, `declines`.
- **Evidence:** High trend persistence (strength >= 60%), price holding above/below VWAP within 1.5x ATR, strong option OI expansion, breadth expansion. Overextended price (> 1.5x ATR from VWAP) is strictly blocked.

## 4. Status Lifecycle & Transition Rules

1. **`RAW`**: Initial candidate generated by detector before qualification.
2. **`QUALIFIED`**: Structurally valid setup meeting baseline evidence rules.
3. **`TRADE_READY`**: Structurally valid AND actionable now (fresh data, acceptable extension, adequate options/liquidity, no blockers).
4. **`WATCHING`**: Promising setup under observation due to minor soft blockers or lower conviction.
5. **`BLOCKED`**: Temporary data/provider/session degradation (e.g. stale feeds, missing options chain, market closed, detector input requirements unfulfilled).
6. **`REJECTED`**: Structurally invalid or low conviction setup (quality score < 45 or severe conflicting signals).
7. **`INVALIDATED`**: Price breached invalidation level (stop loss).
8. **`EXPIRED`**: Market session closed or setup validity window elapsed.

---

## 5. Scoring & Qualification Methodology

Every opportunity undergoes multi-component scoring:
- **`quality_score` (0–100):** Weighted combination of setup quality, structure, trend alignment, options confirmation, breadth, and R:R quality minus extension and conflict penalties.
- **`confidence_score` (0–100):** Weighted combination of detector evidence, data quality, options positioning, and volatility suitability minus news risk and conflict penalties.
- **`priority_score` (0–100):** Composite ranking score combining quality, confidence, and R:R.

### Centrally Versioned R:R Thresholds (`config_version` 1.0.0)
Minimum Risk:Reward ratios are configured per setup type:
- `BREAKOUT`: 1.5
- `BREAKDOWN`: 1.5
- `PULLBACK`: 1.3
- `REVERSAL`: 1.8
- `MOMENTUM_CONTINUATION`: 1.4
