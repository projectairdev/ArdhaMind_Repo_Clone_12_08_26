# AIR ArdhaMind Roadmap

## Phase 1 — product shell and controlled de-scoping

Establish six read-only workspaces, remove execution/paper exposure, isolate visible mocks, add readiness/failure states and preserve deterministic analytics.

## Phase 2 — canonical runtime

Complete field provenance, canonical state, application-service extraction and full canonical pipeline orchestration.

## Later

Historical intelligence persistence, professional charts and option ladder, external alerts, then bounded OpenAI explanation. Paper simulation may become a separate application. Live execution is not part of the current product roadmap.
