# AIR ArdhaMind Phase 1

The authoritative product specification is [docs/product/PRODUCT_SPEC.md](docs/product/PRODUCT_SPEC.md).

AIR ArdhaMind is a live, read-only NIFTY and NIFTY-options intelligence workstation. It validates real market data, runs deterministic analysis, explains the results, and leaves every trading decision and action to the human operator.
